Mobilepay

To pay school fees via M-Pesa, go to the M-Pesa menu on your phone and select Lipa na M-Pesa, then choose Pay Bill. Enter the school's Paybill number [8888888], then for the account number, enter the school's admin ID (e.g. ADM1234) Enter the amount you wish to pay, then enter your M-Pesa PIN and confirm.
